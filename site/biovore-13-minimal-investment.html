A minimal-investment clear of Biovore tier 13. The first Hit Boost goes on Laviscus, Life Steal goes on Khârn, and the Summon power-ups are taken wherever they are offered. Most battles are lost first with low units: every lost attempt at a battle is grouped into one "Havoc Distractions" card, followed by the card for the clear. When you run out of low units you need a second token to keep advancing.
